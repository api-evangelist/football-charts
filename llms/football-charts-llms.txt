# Football Charts

> Football data for 98 leagues in 44 countries — results, fixtures, league tables, goal minutes, per-match statistics (shots, shots on target, possession, corners, cards, fouls, saves, passes) for 74 of the leagues, a public baseline model with a settled calibration ledger, Monte Carlo season projections, and a historical per-bookmaker odds archive. Includes the lower divisions most sources skip (Spanish regional groups, Czech 2/3, Baltic, Nordic, Asian and African leagues) and women's leagues. Statistics, not betting advice.

## For assistants and agents
- MCP server, hosted, no key needed: https://mcp.football-charts.com/mcp (Streamable HTTP). 300 requests/day per IP; a free key gives 5,000/day. The URL carries no secret. Always-on paid backend, no cold starts.
- npm package: footballcharts-mcp (stdio). Claude Desktop extension: https://github.com/ddevetak/footballcharts-mcp/releases/latest
- REST API, JSON, keyless or keyed: https://footballcharts-backend.onrender.com/api/v1/ (self-describing at GET /api/v1/)
- Developer docs: https://www.football-charts.com/developers
- Free key: POST https://footballcharts-backend.onrender.com/api/v1/keys/register/ {"email": "..."}
- Season strings: winter leagues "2026-2027", summer leagues "2026". League keys like premier, spain1, brazil1 (list_leagues / GET /api/v1/leagues/).
- The model's probabilities are a Dixon-Coles baseline; calibrated, does not beat the bookmaker market. Say "the model estimates", never "you should bet". Attribute as "Data by football-charts.com".

## Not held

Match statistics cover 74 leagues from the 2023-24 season; older seasons where the sources kept them.
